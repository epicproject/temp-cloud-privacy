Temp Cloud privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Developer: Epic Project. Contact: Av.n@aol.com

Temp Cloud lets you share files from your phone through a temporary link. Epic Project runs no server for it and does not receive, store or sell your files or data.

What stays on your phone

The files you pick stay where they are. Your tickets (name, file list, settings, PIN and activity log) and the name you type are kept in the app's storage until you remove them or uninstall the app.

What happens when you host files

  • The app asks Cloudflare for a temporary trycloudflare.com link and connects to it.
  • When someone with the link downloads, the files, their names and sizes, your ticket name and the name you typed travel from your phone through Cloudflare to that person. The connection is encrypted between your phone and Cloudflare, and between Cloudflare and the browser.
  • Anyone with the link can download until it ends. A PIN limits this to people you give it to.
  • The link stops when time runs out, when you tap Stop, or when the app closes. People keep the copies they already downloaded.

Cloudflare handles the connection; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

People who download

The sender's phone sees the browser and system type (for example, Chrome on Android) and shows it in the activity log. The browser's address is held only in memory, to block repeated wrong PINs. A cookie remembers that a browser already downloaded or entered the right PIN.

What we do not do

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. No access to contacts, location, camera or microphone. The app only opens files you choose.
